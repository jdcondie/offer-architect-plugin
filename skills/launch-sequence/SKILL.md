# Launch Sequence Writer

## Trigger Description

Use this skill to write the 10 sales emails sent during a product launch window. Run this after completing the pre-launch sequence — both sequences share one ConvertKit automation.

**Trigger phrases:**
- "Write my launch emails"
- "Help me sell my product by email"
- "Write my product launch sequence"
- "I'm launching a product and need sales emails"
- "Build my email launch sequence"
- "Write my sales sequence"

**Do NOT use for:** welcome emails, pre-launch education emails, or post-purchase onboarding. The launch sequence is pure selling — it fires after the pre-launch sequence ends and the cart opens.

---

## Inputs Required

Before writing, collect:

- **Product name** — exact name as it appears on your sales page
- **Product price** — exact price (and any discount being offered)
- **Launch window** — start date, end date, how many hours until deadline in final emails
- **Features list** — every component of your product (5+ features, each with a "normally costs $X" breakdown)
- **Bonuses** — any limited-time bonuses with dollar values and expiry dates
- **Testimonials** — at least 3-5 customer quotes with names and specific results
- **Deep-dive testimonial** — one customer's full before/after story with specific numbers
- **Target audience description** — who they are, what they're struggling with
- **3 desired outcomes** — what customers achieve with your product
- **Refund policy** — exact wording
- **Sales page URL** — linked in every email
- **Your name / sign-off** — how you sign the emails

---

## Process

### Overview

10 emails total. Sent across the launch window (typically 7-10 days). Each email addresses a different angle:

1. Everything That's Included (value stack)
2. Bonuses (urgency)
3. Testimonial Roundup (social proof + objection handling)
4. Where Would You Be (future-pacing)
5. Testimonial Deep Dive (one hero story)
6. Tale of 2 Archetypes (identity story)
7. Financial Outcomes (money transformation story)
8. If Not Now, Then When? (time objection)
9. Reasons Why Others Have Joined (social proof + objection handling)
10. Last Chance (close)

**Critical rules:**
- Every email needs a **PS.** A PS increases CTR by 30-40%.
- Educate before you sell. Even sales emails should answer at least one "education gap" before pitching.
- Send more emails, not fewer. Unsubscribes from people who won't buy are a feature.

## Automation Setup (ConvertKit)

**Critical:** The Pre-Launch and Launch sequences share ONE ConvertKit automation — not two. The launch sequence is a continuation of the pre-launch automation.

### Step 0A: Install the Click Scoring Automation

Before building the launch automation, install the Click Scoring Automation. This tracks how many times someone clicks your sales page link so you can identify warm leads and trigger the Abandoned Cart Sequence.

- [Click here to open the Click Scoring Automation template](https://app.kit.com/a/48c08b997c) → click "Use This Automation"
- Rename: **{Your Product Name} - Click Scoring Automation**
- Replace red tag (step 1): **"Flag - Clicked {Your Product Name} Buy"**
- Replace green tag (step 2): same tag as above
- Replace custom field in blue filter (step 3): **{offername}_launch_score**
- Update custom fields in steps 5 and 6 with the same field name
- Turn on and test by adding the Flag tag to yourself

Then: add "Link Triggers" to all sales page links in your launch emails. When someone clicks, the tag fires and adds them to the Click Scoring Automation.

### Step 0B: Create the "Purchased" Tag Integration

Anyone who buys must be automatically tagged so they exit the launch sequence.

- Create tag: **Purchased - {Your Product Name}**
- Connect your e-commerce platform to ConvertKit (via ConvertKit App Store or Zapier)

- Rename: **{Your Product Name} - Pre-Launch & Launch Sequences**
- Replace red tag at top: **Flag - Start {Your Product Name} Launch Sequence - {Month} {Year}**
- Set first red event to your **Pre-Launch Sequence start date**
- Replace first green tag: **Status - In {Your Product Name} Launch Sequence - {Month Year}**
- Rename first sequence step: **{Your Product Name} - {Month} {Year} - Pre-Launch Sequence**
- Set second red event to your **Product Launch Sequence start date**
- Rename second sequence step: **{Your Product Name} - {Month} {Year} - Product Launch Sequence**
- Set third red event to **6 hours before your Product Launch Sequence ends**
- Update blue filter custom field to match your "launch score" custom field
- Rename third sequence step: **{Your Product Name} - {Month} {Year} - Product Launch Sequence Last Chance Email**
- Set last red event to **morning after your Product Launch Sequence ends**
- Replace last green tag (same Status tag, but this one REMOVES it)
- Replace the 2 red tags at the bottom:
  - **Opt Out - {Your Product Name} Product Launch Sequence**
  - **Purchased - {Your Paid Product Name}**

### Step 2: Upload Your Launch Emails

Uploading checklist:
- Update "sending delay/timing" for each email
- Copy/paste subject line
- Copy/paste preview text
- Copy/paste the content
- Copy/paste any images
- Add in links (link to your waitlist page in pre-launch emails; link to sales page in launch emails)
- Create "launch score" link triggers on all sales/landing page links
- Add Opt-Out Link to the sequence template (do this once — it applies to both sequences since they use the same template)

### Step 3: Test & Turn On

Testing checklist:
- Review all steps in the flow — especially the dates
- Turn on the automation
- Add yourself to the flow
- Check the flow to confirm you've been added
- Send yourself a "preview" of each email — review from both phone and computer
- Click on all the links
- Once reviewed and confirmed, add your email subscribers to the automation

---

## Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not including a PS in every email.** The PS is where the CTA lands for skimmers. Every launch email needs one. No exceptions.

2. **Not tracking clicks on the sales page.** If you don't install the Click Scoring Automation, you can't identify warm leads or trigger the Abandoned Cart Sequence. Set it up before you turn on the launch automation.

3. **Stopping before the sequence is complete.** Most people don't buy until email 7, 8, or 9. Send all 10. The people who unsubscribe from heavy email during a launch were never going to buy.

## Output Specification

10 complete email drafts, each with:
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Full email body with all {placeholders} filled in
- PS section

Format: One email per section, clearly labeled "Email #1: [Name]" through "Email #10: [Name]"

## Chaining

- **Receives from:** `pre-launch-sequence-writer` — the pre-launch sequence feeds directly into this one inside the same ConvertKit automation. The education from pre-launch does the heavy lifting; these emails close.
- **Passes to:** `abandoned-cart-sequence-writer` — warm leads who click 2+ times on the sales page but don't buy get routed into the abandoned cart sequence (runs in parallel during the launch's final days)
- **Passes to:** `post-purchase-sequence-writer` — anyone who buys exits the launch sequence immediately and enters the post-purchase onboarding sequence
- **Standalone:** No. Run pre-launch first.
