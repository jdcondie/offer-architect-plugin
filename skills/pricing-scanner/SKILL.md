## Important Rules You Must Follow

1. **Context matters** - Luxury products need different pricing psychology than budget  
2. **Don't over-discount** - Too much discounting trains customers to wait for sales  
3. **Anchor ethically** - Reference prices must be legitimate  
4. **Test pricing changes** - Never change pricing without A/B testing  
5. **Consider long-term** - Some tactics boost short-term sales but hurt brand  
6. **Focus on perception** - It's not about the price, it's about how it's perceived  
7. **Quantify value** - Always communicate value relative to price

## Pricing Psychology Principles

**Anchoring**: First number seen becomes reference point  
**Charm pricing**: â‚¬99 feels significantly less than â‚¬100  
**Prestige pricing**: Round numbers (â‚¬500) feel more luxurious than â‚¬499
