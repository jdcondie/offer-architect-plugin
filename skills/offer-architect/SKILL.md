# Offer Architect

Create, refine, and sell offers using 4 proven frameworks.

## Core Philosophy

**Make offers daily. The #1 KPI is Offers Made—not created, MADE.**

Until you fix offer frequency, nothing else matters. Broken links, ugly design, simple systems—if you make an offer every day (sometimes more), you'll win.

| Offer Map | Craft compelling offer copy | Writing sales pages, Google Docs |

## Quick Reference: The Daily Rhythm

**Every Day:**
- Make 1 Front End offer (email + social)
- Post Netflix-style content

**Every Week:**
- Make 1 Back End offer to current clients

**Every 2 Weeks Max:**
- Red Velvet your offer (change something)

**Every Month:**
- Open/close an intake
- Update Protagonist progress

## Hormozi Lens — Grand Slam Offer Integration

When building or refining any offer, run it through these Hormozi filters:

### Value Equation Check
> Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

For every offer, ask:
- **Dream Outcome:** Is it framed as a destination, not a vehicle? ("Look good naked" not "gym membership")
- **Perceived Likelihood:** What proof makes it unreasonable NOT to believe? (cases, guarantee, methodology)
- **Time Delay:** What's the first win within 7 days? (Psychological speed costs nothing.)
- **Effort & Sacrifice:** What's done-for-them vs. DIY? (Supplements outsell gyms 2:1. Same dream, less effort.)

Best operators focus on the denominator (faster, easier, more certain), not the numerator (bigger claims).

### Grand Slam Offer Audit
Before finalizing any offer, check:
1. Is comparison impossible? (Category of one, not "slightly better than competitors")
2. Is the value stack broken into named components with dollar values assigned?
3. Do bonuses address specific objections? (Tools and checklists > more training)
4. Is there a named guarantee? ("The Double-Down Guarantee" not "satisfaction guaranteed")
5. Is there real scarcity or urgency? (Fake limits destroy trust)
6. Does the name follow MAGIC? (Magnetic reason + Avatar + Goal + Interval + Container)

### Pricing Diagnostic
- 80%+ close rate → underpriced 3-4x, raise immediately
- 60-80% → underpriced 2-3x
- 35-40% → sweet spot
- Below 30% → sales process or targeting problem, not price

**Never discount. Add bonuses instead.** Discounting trains customers that prices are negotiable.

### Money Model Sequence
Check that the offer includes all 4 stages:
1. **Attraction offer** (turn strangers into customers, cover acquisition cost)
2. **Upsell offers** (increase revenue per customer immediately)
3. **Downsell offers** (change HOW they pay before changing WHAT they get)
4. **Continuity offers** (recurring revenue, long-term relationship)
